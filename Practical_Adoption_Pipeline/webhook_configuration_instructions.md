1. Go to your repository on GitHub.
2. Hit the "Settings" button in your repository's top navigation bar.
3. Hit the "Webhooks" button in the navigation bar on the left side of the page. The form to add a webhook will then appear on the page.
4. Enter your server's endpoint in the "Payload URL" field.
5. Under the "Which events would you like to trigger this webhook?" field, select the "Let me select individual events.". Then, select the "Issues" event.
6. Hit "Add webhook" at the bottom of the form.
